Under the hood

How it's built

Hectic Software Ltd builds natively for Apple, the way Apple builds its own apps. No templates, no app-builders, no third-party tracking code.

Native

Swift & SwiftUI

Apple's own language and design framework. Fast, smooth, and supports dark mode, large text and VoiceOver out of the box.

On-device AI

Apple Intelligence

Fern runs on Apple's on-device model. Private, works offline and costs nothing per question.

Camera

AVFoundation & Vision

Live face tracking, lighting checks, auto-capture and on-device skin analysis.

Health

HealthKit & WeatherKit

Sleep, water, stress and UV from Apple Health and Apple Weather, read on the phone only.

Everywhere

Watch, widgets, Siri

WatchKit, WidgetKit and App Intents put the clinic on the wrist, Lock Screen, Control Centre and Siri.

In the clinic

Phone to iPad

Check-in uses Apple's encrypted local connection, so diaries move phone to iPad with no internet.

Privacy

Private by design

Data stored on the device with SwiftData, protected with the Keychain. Apple's privacy rules built in from day one.

Cloud

Apple iCloud

CloudKit sends your clinic news to every client with no server to run or pay for.

Payments

Apple Pay & Stripe

Secure checkout with Apple Pay, cards and Klarna. StoreKit for in-app subscriptions.

Accounts

Where the app lives: two options

Every app on the App Store sits under a developer account. You choose whose.

Option A · simplest

On Hectic Software's account

  • Nothing for you to set up or pay to Apple.
  • Romeo handles releases, updates and Apple's checks.
  • The App Store shows Hectic Software Ltd as the developer, with your app name and brand.
  • Can be moved to your own account later.
Option B · you own it all

On your own Apple account

  1. Get a free D-U-N-S number for Fresh Face (Apple checks businesses with it). Takes a few days: apple.com D-U-N-S
  2. Join the Apple Developer Program as an organisation (£79 a year): developer.apple.com/programs/enroll
  3. In App Store Connect, go to Users and Access and invite Romeo as an Admin.
  4. Romeo moves the app across. The App Store then shows Fresh Face London as the developer.

Payments

Setting up Stripe

Stripe is how the app takes card, Apple Pay and Klarna payments. Money goes into your Stripe account, then to your bank. Free to open, no monthly fee.

Step 1

Open an account

Sign up at stripe.com with the clinic's email.

Step 2

Add your business

Business type, company or sole trader details, the clinic address and your bank account for payouts. Have your photo ID ready; Stripe verifies who you are.

Step 3

Turn on Klarna

Settings → Payment methods → Klarna → Turn on. Apple Pay works automatically.

Step 4

Invite Romeo

Settings → Team and security → Add member → Romeo's email → role Developer. He connects the app. He never sees your bank details.

Fees are per payment only: 1.5% + 20p on UK cards, Klarna from 4.99% + 35p (Stripe pricing).

Good questions

What you might be wondering

Can I stop using Fresha?Yes. Clients book, pick a therapist and agree your policy in the app, and you manage the diary on the iPad. Move across in your own time; you can run both side by side at first.
What happens to my existing clients?Send them the App Store link once it's live, by text, email, Instagram or a QR code at reception. Clinic news and the invite-a-friend code help spread it.
What do clients pay?Nothing. The app is free to download. They pay only for treatments and products.
Can I change prices, treatments or photos?Yes. Send the changes to Romeo and they go into the next update. Clinic news you can post yourself any time from the iPad.
What if a client has no iPhone?They can still book through your website or by phone. The app is for the clients who want the full experience.
Is it GDPR-friendly?Yes. Diaries, photos and health data stay on each client's own phone, clients can export or delete everything, and the app has a privacy policy.
Who looks after updates?Hectic Software Ltd keeps the app working with each new iPhone and iOS version. What's included is agreed when we meet.
How long until launch?Once you sign off and send the checklist items, Romeo submits it to Apple. Apple's review usually takes a day or two.
Do my staff need anything?Just an iPhone or iPad. For the clinic tools, the front-desk iPad goes into staff mode with a passcode.
