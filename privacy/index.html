Privacy policyUpdated 15 October 2026

Privacy.

Short version: we collect only what we need to build your app with you, we never sell it, and there is no tracking.

Who we are

Hectic Software Ltd, London ("Hectic", "we"). Contact: hello@hecticsoftware.com.

What we collect, and why

Your account. When you sign in to the Hectic app with Apple, Google or email, your name, email, company and phone are stored on your device and in your own iCloud so they sync to your other devices. We do not keep a copy on our servers. If you use Sign in with Apple you can choose to hide your email.

Briefs and projects. When you send a brief (in the app or on this website) we receive the details you enter: name, email, optional company and phone, and your project description. Messages and files you add to a project are stored with it so we can work on it with you. This is held on Cloudflare's infrastructure.

Ask Hectic. Questions you type into Ask Hectic are sent to an AI model run on Cloudflare to produce an answer. They are not used to identify you and are not kept as part of a profile.

Notifications. Reply notifications are created on your device; we don't run an advertising or tracking service.

What we don't do

No advertising, no tracking across apps or websites, no selling or sharing of your data with data brokers.

How long we keep it

Project records are kept while we work together and for as long as needed for accounts and legal obligations, then deleted.

Your rights

You can delete your account in the app (Account → Delete account), which removes your profile and saved projects from the app and your iCloud. To access, correct or erase project records we hold, email hello@hecticsoftware.com. Under UK GDPR you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Client pages

Private client pages are protected by an access code and are not indexed by search engines.
