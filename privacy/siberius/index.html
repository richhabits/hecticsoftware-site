Privacy Policy

Siberius Recovery & Wellness Journal
Published by Hectic Software Ltd · Updated 1 November 2026

Siberius is built around strict data privacy and local-first data ownership. Your personal notes, wellness tracking, and recovery journals remain entirely under your control.

1. Local Encrypted Storage

All journal entries, recovery metrics, and notes are encrypted and stored locally on your device or in your personal iCloud vault. Siberius does not operate central database servers that store or read your entries.

2. Zero Data Collection

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. Siberius does not track your location, read your device contacts, access your photos, or inspect personal identifiers.

3. Analytics & Third-Party Services

Siberius contains no third-party tracking SDKs, no advertising frameworks, and no marketing analytics. There is zero profiling or data brokerage.
