Privacy Policy

Black Moss & Herbs (iOS Application)
Published by Hectic Software Ltd · Updated 1 November 2026

Black Moss & Herbs is committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This policy describes how our mobile application handles user information.

1. Data Collection and Usage

The Black Moss & Herbs iOS application does not collect, track, or share any personal user data. You can browse wellness resources, herbal product catalogs, and traditional guides without creating an account or providing personal details.

2. Analytics and Advertising

We do not use any third-party tracking frameworks, advertising IDs (IDFA), or telemetry SDKs. No personal information is sold, rented, or shared with data brokers or commercial advertisers.

3. Purchases and Web Links

Product orders or consultation bookings initiated through external web links are fulfilled securely on our website (blackmossandherbs.com) using standard encrypted web payment processing. Payment information is handled directly by licensed payment processors and is never accessed or stored by the iOS app.
