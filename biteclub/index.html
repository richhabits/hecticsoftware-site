What you might be wondering

FAQ

Do we have to pay anything?

No. Everything here is a gift from Hectic Software, and it runs on free plans, so there's no monthly bill.

Who owns the website and domains?

You do. The site, domains and email are in Bite Club's own Cloudflare and IONOS accounts.

When does the app go on the App Store?

Once you've confirmed your hours, prices and Terms of sale and tested it, we submit it under the Bite Club name.

Can members see the studio tools?

No. They unlock only on the founders' phones with Face ID and your iCloud account.

How many people can test the app?

Up to 100 with your beta link, all free.

Who do we call if something breaks?

Message us from your private project page below, or send Share Beta Feedback from the app. Romeo at Hectic Software looks after it.
