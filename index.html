Editorial · The StudioP. 34
Founder's letter

Craft is the whole point.

By Romeo Valentine, Founder

Most apps are built to be finished. We build them to be used every day by people who notice the details: how a screen settles into place, how quickly it opens, how it feels in the hand. That is the difference between software people tolerate and software they love.

So we build the way Apple builds, with Swift, SwiftUI and the frameworks behind every Apple screen. One codebase reaches iPhone, iPad, Watch, Mac, Apple TV and Vision Pro, with widgets, Siri and Live Activities included rather than bolted on.

“You talk to the person building your app. Nothing gets lost in translation.”

There are no account managers between you and the work. You see a working beta on your own phone early, decisions are made on the real thing, and the price is agreed in writing before we start.

And when it's done, it's yours: the app, the App Store listing, the code and your customers' data. We stay on to keep it fast and current with every new iOS.

Direct.

Work with the engineer, not a chain of hand-offs.

Every screen.

All six Apple platforms from one codebase.

Yours.

Your app, listing, code and data. No lock-in.

Private.

On-device AI keeps customer data on the phone.

Clear.

Fixed, written quotes and weekly progress.

Q&AP. 52
How much does an app cost?

It depends on scope. After a short call we send a fixed written quote with what's included, so there are no surprises.

Do I own the app and the code?

Yes. On completion the app, its App Store listing and the source code belong to you.

Do you build Android apps?

We specialise in Apple platforms. Where a project needs Android or web, we plan it in from the start.

How fast can I see something?

You get a working beta on your own iPhone through TestFlight early in the build, and new versions as we go.

Will you look after it after launch?

Yes. Care plans cover updates for each new iOS, fixes and new features.

Can I track my project?

Every client gets a private project page with status, quote, messages and files.
