FAQ

Good questions.

How much does an app cost?

It depends on what the app needs to do. After a short call you get a fixed written quote that lists exactly what's included, so there are no surprises.

Do you build for both iPhone and Android?

Yes. Apple apps are built natively in Swift and SwiftUI, Android apps natively in Kotlin with Material 3. Each feels at home on its platform.

Who will I work with?

Romeo Valentine, the founder, who builds every app himself. There are no account managers in between.

How soon can I see something?

You get a working beta on your own phone early in the build, then new versions as the work progresses.

Do I own the app and the code?

Yes. On completion the app, its store listing and the source code belong to you.

What happens after launch?

Care plans cover updates for each new version of iOS and Android, fixes and new features.
