04 · Why native

Built properly
pays back.

Many apps are built once with cross-platform tools or app builders. We build with Apple's own technology.

Typical app-builder or cross-platform buildHectic Software
FeelLooks similar on every phone, rarely like an Apple appNative controls, animations and gestures people already know
Apple featuresWidgets, Watch, Siri and Vision Pro often limited or missingWidgets, Watch, Siri, Live Activities, Control Centre and more
Speed & batteryExtra layers between your app and the phoneRuns directly on Apple's frameworks
PrivacyThird-party code and tracking often bundled inNo tracking libraries; on-device processing where possible
OwnershipLocked to a platform's subscriptionYour app, your listing, your data
